# PRD — Restaurant IQ 2.0 (hackathon scope)

**Customer:** independent restaurants and 1–5 location operators. **Pain:** every tool reports the past; nobody turns outside-the-walls taste data into a costed, testable next move with a record of why.

**P0 delivered:** landing; no-login synthetic demo; onboarding form (manual menu entry, sample-data option); restaurant 360°; Qloo taste discovery with ≥2 distinct signal families (place attributes, demographics, cross-domain entities, nearby places); opportunity engine with 3 ranked hypotheses + not-recommended + no-viable path; opportunity workspace with "how Qloo changed it"; Decision Lab; Action board with approvals; Business memory; With/Without Qloo toggle; auth + RLS; responsive UI; tests; deploy config.

**P1 (if time):** restaurant comparison, CSV sales import, evidence re-check with quotas, EN/ZH copy, printable decision brief, outcome logging UI.

**Non-goals:** payments, POS replacement, inventory, payroll, auto-posting, scraping, revenue forecasts from affinity.

**Success for judges:** a stranger completes the whole path on a phone, sees sources and labels, changes ≥3 economic inputs, saves a decision and finds it in memory.
